NGC 1300

NGC 1300 is a barred spiral galaxy in the constellation Eridanus, approximately 61 million light-years from Earth. Its massive central bar — stretching over 100,000 light-years — channels gas directly into the nucleus, sustaining star formation and feeding a compact nuclear spiral invisible at optical wavelengths.
